One rule to settle either way: does a single missed morning reset the streak to zero? Strict streaks punish travel and illness — exactly the days the data matters most — and people who break one often stop entirely. A forgiving version (one skip allowed per week, shown as a hollow dot that doesn’t break the run) keeps the habit without the guilt.
