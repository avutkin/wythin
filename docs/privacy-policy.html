Wythin — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

Wythin is an independent app built by one developer. It has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. Your readings are stored on your device and, if you enable sync, on a private server run by the developer. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible

Wythin is developed and operated by Alex Utkin as an independent developer. Contact for any privacy question or request: alexey.ytkin@gmail.com.

Heart-rate sensors and Bluetooth

Wythin uses Bluetooth solely to connect to a compatible heart-rate chest strap (such as a Polar H10) and read measurements from it. Bluetooth is never used to detect your location, to identify nearby people or devices for any other purpose, or to build a profile of you. If you never connect a strap, the app collects no physiological data at all.

Where your data goes

Measurements are held on your device. If you enable sync, they are also sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to a private server operated by the developer on Hetzner Cloud infrastructure. That server is not shared with other products and your data is not sold, rented, or disclosed to advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party processing

When the app generates a written interpretation of your readings, the relevant numbers are sent to OpenAI (model gpt-4o-mini) to produce that text. This is the only third party that ever receives any of your data.

What is sent is limited to the measurements and summaries needed for the interpretation — for example activity type, duration, and heart-rate or variability values and their trends. Your name, email address, and phone number are never sent to OpenAI.

What Wythin does not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no data shared for tracking. The app links no third-party libraries whatsoever.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your Apple Health data. The app does not use HealthKit.
  • No location collection.

Your control over your data

You can delete your data at any time from Settings inside the app. This permanently removes your synced measurements, sessions, activities, and profile from the server. Deleting the app from your device removes the copy held on the device.

To ask what is stored about you, or to have it removed by hand, email alexey.ytkin@gmail.com.

Retention

Synced data is kept until you delete it, either from within the app or by asking. There is no fixed expiry, because the value of the app comes from comparing today against your own history.

Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit using TLS. Access to your records requires a personal access token issued to you. As with any service, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

Children

Wythin is not directed to children and should not be used by anyone under 13.

Beta testing

While Wythin is distributed for testing through Apple TestFlight, Apple additionally collects information about your use of the beta, such as installation and crash data, under Apple's own privacy policy. Feedback you submit through TestFlight, including screenshots, is visible to the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the app or by email if you have provided one.
