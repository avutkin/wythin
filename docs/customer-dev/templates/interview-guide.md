# Interview guide

Thirty to forty minutes. Listen more than talk. Do not pitch. Ask about the past, not the future, except in the last block.

## Before the call

- Read the person's file under `users/`. Know their goals, devices, what they log, and when they last synced.
- Pick the two things in their history you most want explained (a gap, a spike, a practice they repeat).

## Opening (5 min)

- How did you hear about Wythin, and what made you install it?
- What were you hoping it would tell you?

## Their routine (10 min)

- Walk me through the last time you used it. What did you do, what did you look at, what did you do next?
- When do you put the strap on, and when do you take it off? Why then?
- Which screen do you open first?

## Value (10 min)

- What is one thing the app told you that you did not know?
- Did it change anything you do? What?
- Which number or screen do you trust? Which do you not?
- (For lapsed users) When did you stop, and what was happening that week?

## Gaps (5 min)

- What did you expect to find that was not there?
- What did you try to do that did not work?
- What other apps or devices do you use for the same thing, and what do they do better?

## Future (5 min)

- If the app knew you for a year, what should it be able to tell you?
- What would make you recommend it to someone? To whom?
- Would you pay? For what part?

## Closing

- Anything I should have asked?
- Can I come back to you when we ship the thing you asked for?

## After the call

- File the transcript under `interviews/`, fill the summary block, copy feedback items to `feedback-log.md` and the user file, and add cards to `KANBAN.md`.
