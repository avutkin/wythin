3. What the service does

Wythin supports personal wellness practices and review of measurements from compatible sensors. Features can include breathing exercises, session and activity histories, self-reported wellbeing, trends, and requested guidance. A connected sensor can supply ECG, beat-to-beat RR intervals and three-axis movement. Movement can support estimates of activity or body orientation; it is not GPS location.

The proposed full-signal archive preserves supported sensor packets for later processing. Its initial daily processing checks signal quality and calculates descriptive statistics. It does not establish a comprehensive assessment of your nervous system, mood, attention, memory or cognitive ability. A feature described in this draft is not a promise that it is already available.

4. Health and safety limits

Wythin is intended for general wellness, not diagnosis, treatment, emergency response or continuous medical surveillance. It does not replace a qualified clinician or professional assessment. Measurements, inferred states and AI-generated explanations can be incomplete or wrong. Sensor contact, movement, timing, missing recordings and other conditions affect results. A score does not establish a disease, a psychological condition or the cause of a change.

Use exercises within your comfort and capabilities. Stop if an exercise causes discomfort, dizziness, pain or unusual symptoms. Seek appropriate medical care for health concerns. Do not delay necessary care or change prescribed treatment because of an app result. Do not perform attention-demanding or breath-holding exercises while driving or in another unsafe situation. These statements do not remove any legal duties we owe you.
